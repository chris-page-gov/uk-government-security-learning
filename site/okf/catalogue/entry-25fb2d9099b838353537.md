## Public listing abstract

The Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) is a high-level framework developed by the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), the UK's technical authority on cyber security. It represents an industry framework that is used by operators of essential services under the Network and Information Systems regulations as well as more widely across the private sector, including Critical National Infrastructure (CNI) sectors.
