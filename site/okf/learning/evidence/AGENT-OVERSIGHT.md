# Human oversight and stopping unexpected activity

Evidence kind: **local-paraphrase**.

The NCSC describes different levels of human oversight. Where unexpected activity could have significant consequences, it recommends human responsibility, monitoring and the ability to investigate and limit unintended activity. Additional judge agents also need independent evaluation.
