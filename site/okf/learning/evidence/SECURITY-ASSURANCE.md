# 4.2 Assurance

Evidence kind: **source-quotation**.

4.2 Assurance The purpose of assurance is to provide, through a systematic set of actions, confidence to senior leaders and stakeholders that work is controlled and supports secure and successful delivery of policy, strategy and objectives.
