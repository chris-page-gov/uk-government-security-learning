# Where should humans approve, monitor or stop an agent?

## Choose oversight by consequence

**source-guidance**

The AI Playbook requires the necessary oversight and human-in-the-loop processes for high-impact or high-risk output. NCSC guidance discusses levels of oversight and the ability to investigate and limit unexpected activity.

Evidence: [AI-HUMAN-CONTROL](../evidence/AI-HUMAN-CONTROL.md), [AGENT-OVERSIGHT](../evidence/AGENT-OVERSIGHT.md).

## Write the intervention points into the design

**local-analysis**

For a local control design, identify which actions need prior approval, who watches ongoing activity, what triggers escalation and who can revoke permission. Test that a stop reaches the executing system rather than only changing a dashboard.

Evidence: [GOVS005-AUTHORITY](../evidence/GOVS005-AUTHORITY.md), [AGENT-OVERSIGHT](../evidence/AGENT-OVERSIGHT.md).

## Directions for next work

- Name the accountable role and approval route.
- Describe the consequence that triggers a human decision.
- Exercise a revocation scenario and retain the evidence.

## What this does not establish

- The appropriate human intervention model depends on the use case and organisational policy.
