# What evidence would an assurance reviewer need?

## Cover the lifecycle and the risk

**source-guidance**

GovS 005 describes assurance across AI use-case selection, data, privacy, security, ethics, oversight, procurement, monitoring and retirement. GovS 007 explains assurance as systematic confidence-building activity.

Evidence: [GOVS005-AI-LIFECYCLE](../evidence/GOVS005-AI-LIFECYCLE.md), [SECURITY-ASSURANCE](../evidence/SECURITY-ASSURANCE.md).

## Build a reviewable evidence pack

**local-analysis**

A local evidence pack could include the approved purpose and owner, policy and model/tool versions, source provenance, threat and risk work, test results, human decisions, operational monitoring and revocation exercises. Map each item to the claim it supports.

Evidence: [AI-AUDITABILITY](../evidence/AI-AUDITABILITY.md), [AI-ASSURANCE](../evidence/AI-ASSURANCE.md), [AGENT-OVERSIGHT](../evidence/AGENT-OVERSIGHT.md).

## Directions for next work

- Ask the relevant assurance team to agree the evidence questions early.
- Keep negative cases and unresolved issues alongside successful tests.
- Distinguish a document review from an operating-control test.

## What this does not establish

- Required evidence and acceptance authority must be agreed for the actual service.
